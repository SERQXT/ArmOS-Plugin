Delivery Methodology
Domo Momentum — How Every Solution Is Delivered

Every solution in this engagement is executed using Domo Momentum, our proven nine-stage delivery methodology. The framework opens with disciplined alignment (Kickoff through Data Strategy), drives value through iterative agile cycles (Build, Test, Review) that release working capability every sprint, and closes with the structural rigor — Governance and Deployment & Adoption — that makes the platform durable and self-sustaining for {{ client.short_name|default(client.name) }}'s team. This is the same methodology behind Domo's most successful enterprise transformations.

Align
Stages 1–4 establish scope, success criteria, the metrics that matter, and the data strategy that powers them — the foundation every solution is built on.
Iterate
Stages 5–7 run as agile sprint cycles — Build, Test, Review — releasing working capability every sprint and absorbing feedback before it compounds into rework.
Operationalize
Stages 8–9 lock in governance, security, and the adoption motion that makes Domo a self-sustaining capability {{ client.short_name|default(client.name) }}'s team owns and operates independently.
