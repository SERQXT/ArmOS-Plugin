Strategic Context
A Platform Transformation, Not a Point Solution

PHA's data infrastructure challenge is not a single problem — it is a collection of interconnected constraints that have compounded over time, limiting the organization's ability to serve its members with the timeliness and depth of insight they deserve. Today, PHA's data team operates across a fragmented stack: PHI clinical data submitted through ad-hoc file transfers, financial and operational data collected via manual spreadsheet intake and email coordination, QA and ETL work managed through a combination of SQL scripts, Microsoft Access, Data 360, and Tableau — each requiring significant manual intervention to move data from receipt to report.

The result is a reporting cycle that, in some cases, stretches 12 to 18 months from data collection to published report — a lag that has created real concerns among PHA's membership about the relevance and value of the organization's analytical output. PHA leadership has identified closing this gap as the single most important outcome this engagement must deliver.

Domo addresses this not by replacing one tool with another, but by providing an end-to-end platform — data integration, transformation, AI-powered validation, warehousing, and visualization — under a single, unified environment. This consolidates the existing vendor landscape (Tableau, AWS, Data 360, Access, and custom-built portals) into one governed, scalable system that PHA's team can own and operate independently over time.

Immediate Priority
Solution 1 — QHR Intake Portal: The Right First Step

Within the broader platform vision, Solution 1 has been deliberately scoped to address PHA's most pressing near-term need: establishing a permanent, Domo-native channel for members to submit their QHR PHI data — before the current collection cycle requires PHA to direct members to a temporary solution.

The decommissioned QHR portal left PHA without a dedicated submission channel. An interim SharePoint site has been prepared as a bridge, but directing members there — only to transition them again to Domo within the same year — would create unnecessary friction and require communicating a process change twice in a short period. By standing up the Domo intake portal first, PHA makes one clear, intentional announcement to its membership: this is the new platform, and this is how things will work going forward.

This approach also ensures that every subsequent solution — AI validation, QHR taxonomy mapping, analytics dashboards, and non-PHI form ingestion — is built on a foundation that members are already familiar and comfortable with, making each new capability feel like a natural evolution rather than a disruption.

Solution 2 — Agentic QHR Validation & Taxonomy Engine Impact
AI validation agent · Human-in-loop workflow · QHR crosswalk · EHR pathway routing · Top 3 EHR systems
Weeks 4–12158 hrs▼

With files landing in Filesets from Solution 1, this solution adds intelligence: an agentic review layer that validates data quality, maps to PHA's QHR taxonomy, and surfaces issues to human reviewers before data enters the analytics pipeline.

Deliverables 7
  • Agentic AI Validation Pipeline ▼
    What it includes
    An AI agent that reviews every uploaded Fileset against the QHR required-field checklist, automatically flagging missing, malformed, or out-of-range data before it enters the analytics pipeline.
    Who it's for
    PHA program operations staffMember-org data submitters
    Outcome & value
    Catches data-quality issues that today only surface 9–12 months later in QHR review — collapsing the remediation cycle from quarters to days and protecting the integrity of every downstream report.

    What it includes
    A workflow-driven review interface where PHA staff inspect agent findings and approve or reject each data packet before it enters the analytics pipeline. The review queue is filtered to exceptions only, not every submission.
    Who it's for
    PHA program operations staff
    Outcome & value
    Keeps PHA staff judgment in the loop where it matters (edge cases, vendor-specific anomalies) while automating the 80% of submissions that are clean — converts staff effort from data janitorial work to exception handling.

    What it includes
    Automated mapping of EHR-specific coding values into PHA's standardized QHR taxonomy. Built on Aaron Cole's existing Data 360 crosswalk scripts and QA logic, ported into Domo with AI-assisted translation so existing institutional knowledge is preserved rather than rebuilt.
    Who it's for
    Aaron Cole (Data 360 owner)PHA program operations staff
    Outcome & value
    Preserves five years of institutional crosswalk work and removes the manual Access database / spreadsheet mapping step that today sits between submission and report. The QHR taxonomy becomes a live capability rather than an annual manual exercise.
  • EHR Pathway Routing — Top 3 Vendors ▼
    What it includes
    Vendor-aware routing for the three EHR systems that cover ~80% of member submission volume. Each pathway carries the validation rules and transformation logic correct for that vendor's data shape.
    Who it's for
    PHA program operations staffMember organizations on Big 3 EHRs
    Outcome & value
    Delivers immediate clean validation for the membership majority. The long-tail vendors are picked up additively in Solution 5 — no re-architecture required when coverage expands.
  • Dynamic Column Mapping Intelligence ▼
    What it includes
    AI detection of EHR column-name changes when a vendor pushes an update, with human approval prompts and confirmed-mapping training so the engine learns from each approved decision.
    Who it's for
    PHA program operations staffMember-org IT contacts
    Outcome & value
    Eliminates the recurring fire drills triggered by EHR vendor updates — historically a major source of QHR cycle delay — and lets the validation engine evolve with the EHR market without consultant rework.
  • EHR Transition Dual-Pathway Handling ▼
    What it includes
    Workflow support for member organizations migrating between EHR systems mid-year, ingesting data from both source and target systems without data loss or double-counting through the transition window.
    Who it's for
    Member organizations mid-EHR-migrationPHA program operations staff
    Outcome & value
    Removes a quiet failure mode in today's manual process, where member orgs in EHR transition either submit incomplete data or are quietly excluded from a QHR cycle entirely.
  • Field-Level Member Feedback Loop ▼
    What it includes
    Automated notifications back to the submitting member organization when validation issues are detected, with field-level remediation guidance ('Field X failed range check; expected range Y–Z') so the org can self-correct without contacting PHA.
    Who it's for
    Member-org data submitters
    Outcome & value
    Member organizations learn from their own submissions in real time, building data-quality maturity inside the membership and reducing inbound 'why was this rejected?' inquiries to PHA staff.

Member Rollout Strategy
Sequenced Pilot Approach — Change Management by Design

Mia emphasized that PHA is a member-facing organization and that member experience cannot be compromised. A phased pilot with the most eager or technically capable member orgs — before full rollout — directly addresses this concern and builds confidence at every level of the organization.

01
Select Pilot Cohort
PHA identifies 5–10 member orgs: the most technically capable, the most eager to submit, or the most trusted to provide candid feedback. These become co-designers of the experience, not just early adopters.
02
Pilot & Iterate
Pilot orgs complete the full upload experience in the Domo portal. PHA and Domo collect feedback, identify friction points, and refine the experience before broader rollout. Members feel heard and valued.
03
Sequenced Rollout
Next cohorts onboarded in tranches — 10 to 25 orgs at a time. Issues from the pilot are already resolved. Confidence builds across the membership. No big-bang cutover, no single point of failure.
Member Communication Strategy
Guiding Principles
One Transition, Not Two
Members are told once: here is the new platform, and here is how you will submit data going forward. No interim step, no second announcement when Domo is ready. Derek confirmed this is the goal.
Iterative Reveal
Members first see the upload-only portal. Over time, they log in to find new capabilities — dashboards, benchmarks, quality feedback. Change feels incremental and additive, not disruptive.
Historical Context Honored
Long-tenured members have years of context with how PHA has operated. Communication should acknowledge the change, explain the benefit, and provide clear documentation for both the process and the platform.
Documentation First
PHA staff must be equipped to field member questions confidently before rollout. Domo will deliver member-facing guides and PHA staff reference documentation alongside each solution launch.
ACE Support — Change Management Accelerator
PHA's included ACE support package (80 hours/year, 20 hours/quarter) provides direct access to Domo's senior engineers for co-building, training, and platform guidance. During Solution 1 rollout, the ACE team can work concurrently with the implementation team — helping PHA staff learn the platform in real time. Recommended structure: regular office hours blocks (e.g., Monday/Wednesday) so the full data team can participate efficiently without consuming implementation time.
